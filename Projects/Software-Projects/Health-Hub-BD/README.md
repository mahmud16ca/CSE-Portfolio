# Health-Hub~BD 🏥

## A Patient-Centred Hospital Management Platform for Bangladesh

## Project Overview

Health-Hub~BD is a bilingual, role-based hospital management system developed to improve healthcare service management in Bangladesh.

The system connects patients, doctors, reception staff, hospital administrators, laboratory staff, and operational departments through one integrated digital platform.

The project focuses on solving common healthcare management problems such as:

- Manual patient records
- Long waiting queues
- Appointment management difficulties
- Billing transparency issues
- Limited digital accessibility

The complete patient workflow is supported:

Registration → Appointment → Smart Queue → Consultation → Prescription → Laboratory → Billing → Follow-up

---

# Project Purpose

The main objectives of Health-Hub~BD are:

- Digitize patient healthcare records
- Reduce manual paperwork
- Improve appointment management
- Reduce waiting time uncertainty
- Provide transparent billing records
- Support Bangla and English healthcare access
- Improve communication between patients and healthcare providers
- Maintain secure healthcare workflows

# Main Features

## 1. Patient Management System

Features:

- Patient registration
- Profile management
- Medical history storage
- Allergy records
- Emergency information
- QR emergency health card
- Feedback and complaint system


## 2. Appointment Management

Features:

- Online appointment booking
- Doctor selection
- Department selection
- Schedule management
- Appointment status tracking
- Walk-in patient registration


## 3. Smart Queue Management

Features:

- Automatic token generation
- Queue status tracking
- Estimated waiting time
- Reception queue management
- Doctor queue control


## 4. Doctor Management

Features:

- Doctor dashboard
- Patient appointment viewing
- Consultation records
- Digital prescription creation
- Follow-up management


## 5. Laboratory Management

Features:

- Laboratory test orders
- Test status tracking
- Report management
- Patient report access


## 6. Billing and Payment System

Features:

- Invoice generation
- Cost estimation
- Payment records
- Payment history tracking

Supported payment records:

- bKash
- Nagad
- Card
- Cash


## 7. Role-Based Authentication

Supported users:

- Patient
- Doctor
- Receptionist
- Administrator
- Hospital Staff

Each role has separate dashboard access and permissions.
